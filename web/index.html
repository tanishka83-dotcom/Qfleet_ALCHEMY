QFleet: Maritime Fleet Fuel Optimisation

SIH Problem ID: SIH26138 — AI/ML & Quantum-Inspired Optimisation for Maritime Fleet Fuel Efficiency and Emissions

How It Addresses the Problem

  1. Predicts fuel use from voyage conditions with a surrogate model (synthetic data)
  2. Assigns vessel, speed and fuel to each voyage to minimize cost, emissions and delay
  3. Compares alternative fuels under a carbon price
  4. Checks every plan against CO2 cap, class quotas, cargo capacity and deadlines, and reports when a method fails them
  5. Benchmarks quantum-inspired methods against an exact solver, including where they lose
