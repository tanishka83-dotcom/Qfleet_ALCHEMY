How It Addresses the Problem

  1. Surrogate Fuel Prediction: Predicts fuel use from voyage conditions with a surrogate model (synthetic data).
  2. Multi-Objective Assignment: Assigns vessel, speed, and fuel to each voyage to minimize cost, emissions, and delay.
  3. Alternative Fuel Evaluation: Compares alternative fuels under a carbon price.
  4. Rigorous Constraint Auditing: Checks every plan against CO₂ cap, class quotas, cargo capacity, and deadlines, and reports when a method fails them.
  5. Classical Quantum-Inspired Benchmarking: Benchmarks quantum-inspired methods against an exact solver, including where they lose.

Engineering Limitations & Disclosures

  • All voyage and fleet data is synthetic (parameterised physics generator).
  • 30 TODO_VERIFY items remain unresolved (flagged in config.py).
  • MILP finds proven optima on all tested instances at this benchmark scale.
  • QI-EA and SQA produce no feasible plans on Medium and Large instances under unassisted heuristic search.
  • QI-EA, GA, and SQA are quantum-inspired classical algorithms — no quantum hardware or qubits are used.
