Engineering Disclosures & Limitations

Transparent reporting of assumptions, computational boundaries, and algorithm constraints.

  • All voyage and vessel telemetry is synthetic data generated from parameterized hydrodynamic models.
  • 38 TODO_VERIFY assumption entries remain recorded and verifiable via API.
  • MILP (exact) wins on all tested benchmark instance sizes; quantum-inspired methods (QI-EA, SQA) lose or fail binding constraints unless assisted by domain repair.
  • Quantum-Inspired Classical Algorithms: GA, SQA, and QI-EA run on standard classical CPUs — there are no physical qubits, superposition, or quantum hardware.
  • Emissions caps and class quotas are strictly enforced; no quantum advantage is claimed.
